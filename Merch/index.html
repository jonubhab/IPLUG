Design guidelines

rules.md

The design must be created using solid colours only. Keep the work minimal and clean.

Specify the intended background color for T-shirts, hoodie and mouse pads.

It must incorporate the club name, IPLUG or the IPLUG logo, clearly or subtly. Minor modifications or redraws are permitted, but major alterations are not.

Submitting designs in high-resolution vector formats (SVG, AI, EPS, PSD) is highly preferred.

Usage of AI is permitted under the theme AI Art. Do mention in the form if AI has been used in design.

If you have a brilliant concept but lack the software skills to render it, you can submit a detailed concept description.

Item-specific instructions

1

T-Shirt

You may place designs on the chest or back, ensuring a high contrast with the garment color.

2

Hoodie

Place the design only on the back as the front side would be zippered. You may place logos or small arts in the front.

3

Mouse Pad

The design will be printed on a rectangular surface. Please ensure crucial visual elements and texts are kept away from edges to account for printing variations.

4

Stickers

Include clean cut lines for the stickers and mention the intended size for the sticker. The design must be legible, impactful and maintain clarity when scaled dramatically.

5

Badges

Center key elements for circular badges as the outer edge will be wrapped or cut off. The design must be legible, impactful and maintain clarity when scaled dramatically.
